Security & your data

You hand us your systems. Here's how we protect them.

How we work inside your company

  • Least-privilege access — named accounts, only the systems named in the contract, reviewed monthly.
  • Your data stays yours — automations run inside your own tools, and client data is never used to train shared models.
  • Secrets in a vault — passwords and API keys live in an encrypted vault, never in chats, sheets or email.
  • Every run logged — what ran, when, and on whose data — auditable by you at any time.

NDA before access

We sign your NDA (or ours) before we touch a single system, and every access is revoked the day an engagement ends.

Clean exit, guaranteed

Every automation ships with SOPs and documentation. If we ever part ways, your systems keep running and your team can operate them — nothing is held hostage.

Proof, not promises

Access reviews, run logs and impact reports are part of your monthly reporting, so the proof lands in your inbox — not as a promise in a slide deck.
