Security & your data

You hand us your systems. Here's how we protect them.

Least-privilege access

Named accounts, only the systems named in the contract, reviewed monthly. Passwords and API keys live in an encrypted vault — never in chats, sheets or email.

Your data stays yours

Automations run inside your own tools, and client data is never used to train shared models. Every run is logged — what ran, when, and on whose data — auditable by you at any time.

NDA & a clean exit

We sign your NDA (or ours) before we touch a single system, and every access is revoked the day an engagement ends. Every automation ships with SOPs and documentation — nothing is held hostage, and access reviews, run logs and impact reports land in your monthly reporting as proof, not promises.
